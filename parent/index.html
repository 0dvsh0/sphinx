If anyone asks them about this game

Only play with someone whose grown-up you already know. No matter what a stranger tells you, get back to your own grown-up and tell them.

Know Your Location

Know Your Location is off. If you turn it on, the walk's start point and a button are shared with a grown-up you choose. Nothing is sent until you press the button, and no photos, riddles or answers are ever included.

Reading

Increases letter and word spacing. Letter shapes are unchanged, because loading a dyslexia-specific typeface from the internet would send this child's browsing data to a company we do not control, and would break when there is no signal.
