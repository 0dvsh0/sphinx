What leaves this device

Nothing from the child's phone. Ever. There is no account, no sign-in, and no identifier of any kind.

The only two requests Sphinx makes

Both happen when a grown-up builds a walk, and both go from our server to a public service. A child playing a saved walk makes no requests at all - that is what the offline mode is for.

  1. A place name

    Sent to Nominatim to turn “Bristol” into coordinates. Needed because that is how you ask a map what is in a place.

  2. A bounding box

    Sent to Overpass to list the things inside it. Needed for the same reason.

Never sent, from the phone

  • ✕Photos. The photo check happens on the device; the image is never uploaded and never stored.
  • ✕Location. Used to tell a grown-up where the walk started, on the device, and never transmitted unless you turn on Know Your Location and press the button.
  • ✕Riddles and answers.
  • ✕Your name, age, birthday, email or phone number. We ask for an age band and store it in your browser.
  • ✕Analytics or telemetry. There is none.
  • ✕Anything to a third-party CDN. Fonts, scripts and models are all served from this origin, because a CDN is someone else learning who is playing.

Why that matters more than usual here

This app is for children. A photograph of a child standing at a memorial, or a history of where they walk after school, is a record of a child's movements. Keeping it on the device is not a feature we added for convenience - it is the design. There is no server-side copy to leak, subpoena or breach.
