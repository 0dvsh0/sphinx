SIMPLE, SAFE ARCHITECTURE

How Sphinx Works

Engineered for children and families. Everything is designed to make screen time as short as possible.

01

Give it Any Place

Sphinx queries the OpenStreetMap database for real, physical memorials, blue plaques, drinking water fountains, and sculptures in your area.

Adaptive radius sweeps 500m to 2.2km until enough candidate objects exist.
02

Adult Reads Every Riddle

A parent reviews every generated riddle and historical fact on one screen. Nothing starts until a grown-up approves the walk.

Under-12 age gate & automated wayfinding lints protect every stop.
03

Walk With Signal Off

The entire walk is stored offline in IndexedDB. Turn on airplane mode, put the phone in your pocket, and walk to the target together.

Pocket mode dims screen to near-black after 20s to prevent walking-while-scrolling.
04

Local Photo Check

When you find the object, snap a photo. An on-device CLIP model checks it right in your browser. Zero photos are ever uploaded.

Transformers.js WebAssembly inference ensures complete offline privacy.
THE PEDAGOGICAL TWIST

Playing the game fixes the map of Earth.

What happens when a riddle leads to a drinking water fountain that was removed last year?

The player taps “It’s gone”. Sphinx files a polite, structured OpenStreetMap Note for volunteer cartographers.

“You found something the map got wrong.”

Never framed as a player failure. It teaches children that the map is made by human beings and can be improved.

Draft OpenStreetMap NoteHuman review required

Drinking water reported missing at 51.4503° N, 2.5971° W.

“The drinking water mapped here was checked on foot on 2026-10-11. It could not be located.”

Privacy Guarantee: No photo, no child name, and no account is ever attached. A grown-up confirms and sends it.
CHILD SAFETY & DATA INTEGRITY

Why Parents Can Say Yes

We believe games for children should respect their physical safety, privacy, and attention.

Provable Privacy

  • • Photos & GPS never leave the smartphone.
  • • No accounts, no logins, and zero advertising cookies.
  • • No remote fonts, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.
Read the verifiable privacy audit →

Physical & Stranger Safety

  • • Automated lint forbids movement instructions (“cross road”, “turn left”).
  • • Target objects must be on public footpaths, never private land.
  • • Built-in safety script protects against stranger approaches.
View Parent Safety Guide →

Anti-Engagement by Design

  • • No streaks, badges, confetti, or XP animations.
  • • No infinite scrolling or push notifications.
  • • Screen dims to pocket mode after 20 seconds.
Metric: time-to-pocket, not time-in-app

Install Sphinx on Your Device

Sphinx is a Progressive Web App (PWA). It installs directly from your web browser, saves walks to local device memory, and runs without internet in airplane mode.

Sphinx is currently distributed as an open PWA. Native store packages are in review.

iOS Safari: Tap the Share button in Safari, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Sphinx will appear as an app icon and launch in full-screen offline mode.
